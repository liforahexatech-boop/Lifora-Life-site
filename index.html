Intelligent Emergency Healthcare

Treatment Shouldn't Wait For Documentation.

Lifora is an intelligent emergency healthcare platform designed to reduce delays in patient identification and documentation. For unconscious critical patients, Lifora enables secure identification through biometrics and iris scanning. For non-critical patients, medical details can be accessed quickly through QR-based identification.

EMERGENCY IDENTIFICATION

Choose the Patient Condition

Lifora provides different identification methods depending on the patient's emergency condition.

HIGH PRIORITY

Critical Patient

For unconscious or unresponsive patients who cannot provide consent or medical information.

  • Biometric Identification
  • Iris Scanner Verification
  • Instant Medical Record Access
  • Emergency Contact Notification
QUICK ACCESS

Non-Critical Patient

For conscious and stable patients who can be identified quickly using secure QR technology.

  • QR-Based Identification
  • Previous Medical Information
  • Faster Documentation
  • Reduced Waiting Time
AUTOMATED ALERT

Notify Emergency Contact

Automatically inform the patient's emergency contact about their hospital admission and location.

  • Hospital Name
  • Hospital Location
  • Emergency Notification
  • Written Consent Information
SIMPLE EMERGENCY WORKFLOW

From Emergency To Immediate Action.

01

Patient Arrives

The patient reaches the emergency department and requires immediate medical attention.

02

Secure Identification

Critical patients use biometric or iris scanning, while non-critical patients use QR identification.

03

Medical Data Access

Essential patient information and previous medical records become available quickly.

04

Family Is Notified

The emergency contact receives the hospital name and location for written consent.

OUR PURPOSE

Every Second Saved Can Save A Life.

In emergencies, valuable time can be lost collecting patient details, identifying unconscious patients and waiting for documentation. Lifora aims to reduce this delay through intelligent identification and automated emergency communication.

Faster Identification. Faster Information. Faster Care.
